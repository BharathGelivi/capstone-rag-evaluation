Question
Under the BSA, when is a document considered to be in 'proper custody' for the purposes of the presumption of genuineness?
X-RAG's Answer
Under the BSA (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam), a document is regarded as being in **proper custody** for the purpose of the presumption of genuineness when: - It is kept **in the place where it is required to be kept**, and - It is **looked after by the person with whom such document is required to be kept**. Additionally, custody will not be considered improper if it can be shown that the document **had a legitimate origin**, or if the circumstances of the case make that origin probable. This definition is provided in the Explanation to the relevant section (see the passage discussing sections 80 and 92)【f940d7fd-8aa1-4435-ae63-7b1fad4e66a3】.
