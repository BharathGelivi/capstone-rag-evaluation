Question
What is the punishment for abetting the suicide of an ordinary person (not a child or person of unsound mind, delirium, or intoxication)?
X-RAG's Answer
For abetting the suicide of an ordinary person (i.e., not a child, person of unsound mind, delirious person, or intoxicated person), the law prescribes: - **Imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to ten years**, and - **Liability to fine**. This is set out in **Section 108** of the provision, which states: > “If any person commits suicide, whoever abets the commission of such suicide, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to ten years, and shall also be liable to fine.”【4†L13-L15】 (For comparison, abetting suicide of a child, person of unsound mind, delirious person, or intoxicated person carries a more severe penalty under Section 107—death or life imprisonment, or imprisonment up to ten years, plus fine.)
