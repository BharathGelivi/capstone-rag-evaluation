The Case
Question
Under BNSS, if a Magistrate attaches a subject of dispute after finding a case to be one of emergency, when may that Magistrate withdraw the attachment?
X-RAG's Answer
Under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS), a Magistrate who attaches the subject of a dispute after finding the case to be one of emergency may withdraw that attachment **at any time** if he is satisfied that there is no longer any likelihood of a breach of the peace concerning the subject of dispute【6e109610-3ad4-4953-a3d8-9df55d06f072】. This provision is found in the proviso to subsection (1) of section 165 of the BNSS.
