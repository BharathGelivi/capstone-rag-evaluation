Question
Under BNSS, whose powers and duties may be exercised by a successor-in-office, and who resolves doubt about who that successor is?
X-RAG's Answer
Under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), **the powers and duties of a Judge or Magistrate may be exercised or performed by his successor‑in‑office**【ff466593-e128-4b78-b947-3bf56cc05525】. If there is any doubt about who that successor‑in‑office is: - For a **Judge**, the **Sessions Judge** determines, by written order, who shall be deemed the successor‑in‑office. - For a **Magistrate** (including a Chief Judicial Magistrate or District Magistrate), the **Chief Judicial Magistrate** or the **District Magistrate** (as the case may be) makes that determination by written order. These provisions are found in Section 29 of the BNSS.【ff466593-e128-4b78-b947-3bf56cc05525】
