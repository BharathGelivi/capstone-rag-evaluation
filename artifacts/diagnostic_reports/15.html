What additional punishment applies under BNS section 106(2) if a person causes death by rash and negligent driving and then escapes without reporting the incident to police or a Magistrate?
X-RAG's Answer
Under BNS § 106(2), a person who causes death by rash and negligent driving of a vehicle (not amounting to culpable homicide) **and then escapes without reporting the incident to a police officer or a Magistrate** shall be punished with: - **Imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to ten years**, and - **Liable to fine**【13c9bef0-00e1-4778-ab87-e705bfe68f76】. This is the additional punishment prescribed for the escape‑without‑reporting element of the offence.
