The Case
Question
Under BNSS, who may withdraw powers conferred by the High Court or State Government on a person or subordinate officer?
X-RAG's Answer
Under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), the authority that may withdraw powers conferred by the High Court or the State Government is **the High Court or the State Government itself** (as the case may be). Specifically, Section 28(1) states: > “The High Court or the State Government, as the case may be, may withdraw all or any of the powers conferred by it under this Sanhita on any person or by any officer subordinate to it.”【ff466593-e128-4b78-b947-3bf56cc05525】 Additionally, if the powers were originally conferred by a Chief Judicial Magistrate or a District Magistrate, those respective magistrates may withdraw the powers they granted【ff466593-e128-4b78-b947-3bf56cc05525】. Thus, the withdrawing authority mirrors the conferring authority: the High Court or State Government for powers they conferred, and the Chief Judicial Magistrate or District Magistrate for powers they conferred.
