Question
What two types of conduct constitute the offence of 'stalking' under BNS section 78?
X-RAG's Answer
Under **BNS section 78(1)**, the offence of stalking is committed by a man who engages in either of the following two types of conduct: 1. **Following a woman and contacting (or attempting to contact) her to foster personal interaction repeatedly, despite a clear indication of her disinterest** 2. **Monitoring the woman’s use of the internet, e‑mail, or any other form of electronic communication** These are the two distinct behaviours that constitute stalking under the provision. *Source: [Chunk-ID: c9dff0c8-b2f3-4f8c-9bb4-a0f6076a2d77]*
