Question
When does the right of private defence of the body commence, and how long does it continue?
X-RAG's Answer
The right of private defence of the body **commences** as soon as a **reasonable apprehension of danger to the body** arises from an **attempt or threat to commit an offence** (the offence itself need not have been committed yet). It **continues** for as long as that reasonable apprehension of danger to the body persists. [Chunk-ID: 90f8747a-7f40-4ae3-b114-2b216129f432]
