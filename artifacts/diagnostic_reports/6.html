Question
How does the BNS define 'criminal conspiracy'?
X-RAG's Answer
The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) defines **criminal conspiracy** in Section 61(1) as: > “When two or more persons agree with the common object to do, or cause to be done— > (a) an illegal act; or > (b) an act which is not illegal by illegal means, such an agreement is designated a criminal conspiracy.” The definition is further qualified by a proviso that an agreement to commit an offence amounts to a criminal conspiracy only if some act besides the agreement is done by one or more parties in pursuance thereof, and an explanation clarifies that it does not matter whether the illegal act is the ultimate object of the agreement or merely incidental to it. **Source:** Section 61(1) of the BNS, as reproduced in the retrieved context.
