What is the punishment for murder under BNS section 103, and how does it change when a group of five or more persons acting in concert commits murder on grounds such as race, caste, or community?
X-RAG's Answer
**Punishment for murder under BNS § 103** - **§ 103(1)** – Whoever commits murder shall be punished with **death or imprisonment for life**, and shall also be liable to a fine. **When a group of five or more persons acting in concert commits murder on specified grounds** - **§ 103(2)** – If a group of five or more persons, acting together, commits murder on the ground of race, caste, community, sex, place of birth, language, personal belief, or any other similar ground, **each member of the group** shall be punished with **death or imprisonment for life**, and shall also be liable to a fine. Thus, the prescribed punishment for murder does not differ between an individual offender and a group of five or more acting in concert on those grounds; both attract the death penalty or life imprisonment plus a fine. *Source: BNS § 103(1) and (2) as reproduced in the retrieved text*【ac6c1423-8651-4ff8-9f26-0cf720a5f981】.

102. If a person, by doing anything which he intends or knows to be likely to cause
death, commits culpable homicide by causing the death of any person, whose death he

103. (1) Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death or imprisonment for
life, and shall also be liable to fine.
(2) When a group of five or more persons acting in concert commits murder on the

other similar ground each member of such group shall be punished with death or with
imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine.
104.Whoever, being under sentence of imprisonment for life, commits murder, shall be
punished with death or with imprisonment for life, which shall mean the remainder of that
person’s natural life.
105. Whoever commits culpable homicide not amounting to murder, shall be punished
