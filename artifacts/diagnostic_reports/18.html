What is the punishment for attempt to murder under BNS section 109, and how does it change if hurt is actually caused to a person?
X-RAG's Answer
**Attempt to murder under BNS § 109** - **Basic offence (no hurt caused):** Imprisonment for life (the maximum term prescribed for the attempt). *[Source: the table entry for § 109 in the retrieved context, which lists the punishment as “Imprisonment for life, or …”]* - **If hurt is caused to any person:** The punishment is enhanced to **death** or **imprisonment for life** (where life imprisonment means imprisonment for the remainder of that person’s natural life). *[Source: the sub‑section § 109(2) entry: “Attempt by life‑convict to murder, if hurt is caused – Death, or imprisonment for life which shall mean the remainder of that person’s natural life.”]* Thus, the presence of actual hurt upgrades the sanction from a straight life‑sentence to the possibility of death (or life imprisonment).
